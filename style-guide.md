# Front-end Style Guide

## Layout

The designs were created to the following widths:

- Mobile: 375px
- Tablet: 768px
- Desktop: 1024px

> 💡 These are just the design sizes. Ensure content is responsive and meets WCAG requirements by testing the full range of screen sizes from 320px to large screens.

## Colors

### Primary

- Green 200 (lighter): HEX #E0F1E8 | RGB 224, 241, 232 | HSL 148°, 38%, 91%
- Green 600 (medium): HEX #0C7D69 | RGB 12, 125, 105 | HSL 169°, 82%, 27%
- Red (Errors): HEX #D73C3C | RGB 215, 60, 60 | HSL 0°, 66%, 54%

### Neutral

- White: HEX #FFFFFF | RGB 255, 255, 255 | HSL 0°, 0%, 100%
- Grey 500 (medium): HEX #86A2A5 | RGB 134, 162, 165 | HSL 186°, 15%, 59%
- Grey 900 (darker): HEX #2A4144 | RGB 42, 65, 68 | HSL 187°, 24%, 22%

## Typography

### Font

- Family: [Karla](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Karla)
- Weights: 400 (Regular), 700 (Bold)

### Headings

- **Heading**: Karla Bold (700) | Size: 32px | Line Height: 100% | Letter Spacing: -1px

### Body Copy

- **Body MD (Bold)**: Karla Bold (700) | Size: 18px | Line Height: 150% | Letter Spacing: 0px
- **Body MD (Regular)**: Karla Regular (400) | Size: 18px | Line Height: 150% | Letter Spacing: 0px
- **Body SM**: Karla Regular (400) | Size: 16px | Line Height: 150% | Letter Spacing: 0px
